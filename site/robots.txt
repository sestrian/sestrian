# The project's whole claim is that the model and its provenance are public and
# checkable. Blocking the crawlers that would quote it, or the AI systems people
# now ask about it, would contradict that. So: no disallows, and the AI crawlers
# are named explicitly rather than left to a wildcard, because several of them
# treat an unnamed agent as an implied opt-out.
