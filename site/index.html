Today’s best AI belongs to a handful of companies. This one belongs to you — the model lives on the chain itself, trained by machines like yours, rebuilt bit for bit on your own desk. The thing being mined is intelligence.

The deal

The model doesn’t live on our servers — it lives on the public chain, where you can read it, check it, and rebuild it on your own machine. No gatekeeper, no API key, no one who can ever cut you off.

Point your graphics card at the network and it trains the live model. Your gaming PC, your workstation, even your Apple laptop — the client measures your speed and sizes every round to what your hardware can actually finish. No minimum rig, no permission slip.

Train, and you earn. Every block splits its reward between the people whose work actually improved the model, the one who assembled the block, and the owners of the data it learned from. Every payout is public math — you can check your cut yourself, down to the coin.

Get paid by users, not hype.

Most networks pay you in freshly printed tokens and hope they’re worth something later. Sestrian is wired the other way: people pay real money to use the model you train, and the network is built so that money — not a money printer — funds your rewards. New coins kick-start your earnings today; real customers are what’s meant to carry them tomorrow.

The goal on the wall: the first block where users pay in more than the printer mints.

Four steps. The network does the rest.

  1. 01

    Bring your GPU

    One command builds the node, creates your wallet, and rebuilds the starting model on your own machine — so you never have to trust a download.

  2. 02

    Train a round

    Your machine improves the current model on real data, entirely on your own hardware, in a round sized to what your GPU can finish in time.

  3. 03

    Sign your work

    Your machine signs its improvement, stamps it with the data it learned from, and sends it out. Your name is on your work.

  4. 04

    Earn

    The network tests every update on questions it held back, keeps what genuinely helps, and pays you in proportion to how much yours did.

No GPU? Bring data.

Open-weight models are usually built on scraped data. This one pays its sources. Your data isn’t free fuel here — it’s property, and it earns. Put a deposit behind data you own and it starts paying you: a share of every block it helps train, and a cut of the fee whenever the model uses what you taught it to answer someone. Every update names its sources, so the credit reaches you — not whoever scraped you. And if your data turns out to matter years from now, it starts earning then. Usage is the clock, not age.

Two rival GPUs. One mind.

Whatever card you bring, the network agrees with it — exactly. Apple and NVIDIA chips do their decimal math a little differently, so Sestrian rounds every update to whole numbers before it touches the chain — and whole numbers never disagree. We run it live: a MacBook and a gaming PC in different buildings, no referee between them, landing on the same model to the last bit. You never have to trust another machine. Or us.
